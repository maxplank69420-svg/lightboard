Line up the projection

Set the drawing area on the main display first. Then keep this phone still, facing the whole surface.

Keep the phone camera steady.

Mount it where it can see your hand clearly; it can sit closer to the wall than your projector. Keep this page in front. The app will try to keep the screen awake. Locking the phone or switching apps stops drawing.

Camera frames are processed on this phone. Only tracking coordinates and controls go to the paired display over encrypted WebRTC. PeerJS provides online connection signaling and, when needed, a relay. Use the same Wi-Fi for the most reliable connection.
