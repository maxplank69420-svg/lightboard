MAKE THE CONNECTION

Your wall, connected.

Mount the webcam on your projector, facing the wall. Keep the whole projected area and your hand in view.

◉Camera preview

Video stays on your device. No accounts, no uploads.

Keep your index finger at the wall. With pinch mode, touch your thumb to your middle finger to turn ink on.

○ Not calibrated

Line up four points to match the camera to your projection. Recalibrate if the camera or projector moves.

Extended display setup

Choose “Extend” in your computer’s display settings. Move this window onto the projector, enter fullscreen, then calibrate. The app runs inside that display; it does not create a virtual display.

Quick controls & setup tips

P: pen · E: eraser · F: fullscreen · H: hide controls · Ctrl/⌘ Z: undo · Esc: cancel calibration. Mouse and touch also work. Hold your fingertip over a pen, color, eraser, or undo button for a second to select it from the wall. Some room light helps the webcam see your hand against a black canvas. A webcam tracks position, not wall contact.
