Extended display setup

Choose “Extend” in your computer’s display settings. Move this window onto the projector, enter fullscreen, then calibrate. The app runs inside that display; it does not create a virtual display.

Quick controls & setup tips

P: pen · E: eraser (larger size) · Space: marker ink on/off · F: fullscreen · H: hide controls · Ctrl/⌘ Z: undo · Esc: cancel calibration. Mouse and touch also work. Hold your fingertip over a pen, color, eraser, or undo button for a second to select it from the wall. Some room light helps the webcam see your hand against a black canvas. A webcam tracks position, not wall contact.
